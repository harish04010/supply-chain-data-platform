Analytics engineering · dbt + DuckDB · Ridgeline Outfitters (case study)

One version of the supply chain numbers

Purchase orders, vendor ship notices, distribution-center receipts, store shipments, sales and a million-event inventory stream, modeled in dbt into a star schema and certified metrics. Every number below comes from a governed definition, and the pipeline proves its own inputs before anyone sees them.

Ask the data

Questions are answered only from the governed metric definitions, never from free-form SQL. Each answer shows the definition it used and is checked against the certified value before it's displayed.

Vendor performance, connected to the shelf

On-time in-full and fill rate from PO lines joined to receipts; ASN accuracy from what vendors said they shipped versus what arrived; in-stock from inventory of each vendor's SKUs. Stores are buffered by DC stock, so a vendor's problems show up at the DC first: V07 stocks out at the DCs several times more often than anyone else while its store in-stock still looks fine.

Incident: the snapshot feed loaded one day twice

Root cause: the warehouse snapshot extract for DC1 on May 12 was delivered twice. Fix: staging keeps one row per date, location and SKU. Prevention: a source-level audit warns on any repeat load, and the reconciliation test fails the build if snapshots and events ever disagree again.
